    Cowboy Online

    Calder Territory, 1899

    Sixteen square kilometres of plains, pine forest, mountains, high snow country, swamp and desert, a river town and four smaller places, with townsfolk on the streets, riders on the roads and a sheriff in Calder's Landing. You start on the main street with your horse beside you and a revolver and a repeater on you. All of it runs inside this tab.

    Things to try

    • Walk away from your horse, then press Htap Whistle. It comes at a run. Call it from too far off and it is brought in behind you, out of sight, and runs the rest.
    • Gallop with Shiftthe Gallop button. The ring by the map empties; after half a minute the horse is winded and needs its breath back.
    • Gallop at a fence and press Spacetap Jump just before it. Too early or too slow and the horse will not clear it.
    • Draw with 1 or 2, aim with the right mouse button and fire with the leftGun, then hold Aim and tap Fire. With the gun up, QNerve is Iron Nerve: mark targets, then QNerve again fires at each in turn.
    • Shoot someone in town and whoever sees it runs to the sheriff. Stop them, outrun the search, or hold Xhands up and do your time in the cell behind his office.
    • Take the north road into the mountains, or the bridge east and down into the Mirewood. A day lasts 48 minutes, and the weather comes and goes.
