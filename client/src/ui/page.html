Calder Territory

A whole world is running inside this tab: the same server code that will run on a host, with 63 bots walking about in it. Step in and try the netcode. The real map, the horses and the guns come in later phases.

Three things to try

  • Sprint, then throw yourself the other way. The body plants a foot before it turns; it does not spin on the spot.
  • Add 150 ms of ping with L. Your own movement should feel the same, and Prediction off by should stay at 0 mm.
  • Watch the radar. Bots appear at 384 m, and the farther off they are the less often you are told where they are.
