    Cowboy Online

    Calder Territory, 1899

    Sixteen square kilometres of plains, pine forest, mountains, high snow country, swamp and desert, a river town and four smaller places, with townsfolk on the streets and riders on the roads. You start on the main street of Calder's Landing with your horse beside you. All of it runs inside this tab.

    Things to try

    • Walk away from your horse, then press Htap Whistle. It comes at a run. Call it from too far off and it is brought in behind you, out of sight, and runs the rest.
    • Gallop with Shiftthe Gallop button. The ring by the map empties; after half a minute the horse is winded and needs its breath back.
    • Take the north road into the mountains, or the bridge east and down into the Mirewood. A day lasts 48 minutes, and the weather comes and goes.
